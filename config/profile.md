# Research profile (public) — used to write "Impact on me"

Ben-Chi Ma, Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering, University of Notre Dame
(advisor Prof. Tengfei Luo). Research: AI-accelerated multiscale materials
modeling and phonon thermal transport in semiconductors and at interfaces.
Interested in semiconductor / electronics thermal management in industry and
computational-materials / ML research.

Current public projects:

- **GaN/AlGaN HEMT multiscale thermal modeling** — MLIP → phonon properties →
  BTE → device-scale self-heating of GaN power/RF transistors; AlGaN MLIP pipeline.
- **InGaAs alloys** — self-trained Deep Potential models vs MACE for κ across
  compositions; MLPhonon (MLIP-to-lattice-dynamics workflow, public repo);
  part of the multi-institution "Phonon Olympics" comparison.
- **Boron arsenide** — active-learning MLIPs, lattice dynamics, interface transport.
- **Phonon band discretization for deterministic BTE (JAX-BTE)** — frequency ×
  mean-free-path binning that keeps both bulk and interface accuracy.
- **GaN/Al₂O₃ interfaces and dislocation cores** — phonon spectra and
  interfacial thermal conductance.
- **BaTiO₃ dislocations** — phonon direction asymmetry, BTE vs vibrational EELS.
- **SrTiO₃ tilt grain boundaries** — κ decreases with tilt angle.
- **HPC** — CUDA/OpenMP/JAX solvers, HTCondor; GPU-accelerated phonon BTE.

Tools: VASP, LAMMPS, phonopy/phono3py, ALAMODE, DeePMD-kit, MACE, JAX, Python.

Preference: computation and theory first. Experimental papers (synthesis,
fabrication, doping, device measurement) are low priority unless they provide
data or mechanisms a computational study can use directly.

Interests to flag in "Impact on me": methods transferable to the projects
above; MLIP interpretability, latent-space analysis, data efficiency and
failure modes; interfacial thermal transport mechanisms; electronics cooling
and thermal simulation trends relevant to industry jobs; agents and automated
workflows for first-principles / lattice-dynamics / thermal-MD calculations;
venues and calls worth submitting to.
